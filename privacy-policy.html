NexApp ("we", "us", "our") builds NexMusic, a music streaming application available on desktop and mobile. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use NexMusic, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By installing or using NexMusic, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We try to collect only what's needed to make the app work well. Depending on how you use NexMusic, this can include:

  • Account details — such as a display name, email address, or sign-in identifier, if you create an account or connect a third-party sign-in.
  • Usage data — songs played, skips, playlists, search queries, and general app interactions, used to power recommendations and fix bugs.
  • Device & diagnostic data — device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and performance metrics.
  • Content you provide — playlist names, comments, or any other information you choose to enter into the app.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate core features such as playback, search, playlists, and offline downloads;
  • Personalize recommendations, mixes, and radio based on your listening habits;
  • Sync your library and queue across your desktop and mobile devices;
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve app performance and stability;
  • Communicate service updates, security notices, or respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Local storage & offline downloads

When you download a song or playlist for offline listening, that content and related metadata are stored locally on your device. Offline downloads remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app, and are not automatically uploaded to our servers beyond what's needed to verify your access to the content.

4. Cookies & similar technologies

The desktop and mobile apps may use local storage, cached identifiers, and similar technologies (rather than traditional browser cookies) to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how features are used. You can usually clear this data through your device or app settings.

5. Third-party services

NexMusic may rely on third-party infrastructure and services — for example, cloud hosting, crash reporting, analytics, and payment processing where applicable — to operate reliably. These providers process data on our behalf and are only permitted to use it to provide services to us. Some music, artwork, or metadata shown in the app may also be sourced from third-party catalogs or APIs, which have their own terms and privacy practices.

6. How we share information

Aside from the service providers mentioned above, we may share information: to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or valid governmental request; to enforce our Terms & Conditions or protect the rights, property, or safety of NexApp, our users, or the public; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we'll make reasonable efforts to notify you.

7. Data retention & your choices

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, security, or backup purposes. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below; we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe and in line with applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

NexMusic is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we work to protect your data, we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. International users

If you use NexMusic from outside the country where our servers or service providers are located, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a different country. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we'll aim to provide more prominent notice, such as an in-app message.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, reach out to us at privacy@nexapp.example.
